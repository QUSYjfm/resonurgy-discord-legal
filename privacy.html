Privacy Policy

Resonurgy Discord community services · Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Manafold Studio operates the Resonurgy Discord application and ManaFoldBOT, our community ticket and support service. For questions about this policy or your information, contact manafoldstudio@gmail.com.

This policy covers our Discord community services and related support correspondence. It does not describe all data processing by the Resonurgy game, Steam, Discord, or independently operated third-party bots. Our Discord services currently have no direct integration with gameplay or a Discord-based login to the game.

2. Information handled

When you use the ticket service, we process information needed to create, identify and respond to your request. Depending on what you submit and the ticket interaction, this can include:

  • Your Discord user ID, username or display name, and server, channel and message identifiers associated with the request.
  • Ticket messages, bug descriptions, screenshots, files, attachments and other information you choose to submit.
  • Message timestamps, ticket status and staff replies recorded on Discord.

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments. Please avoid sending passwords, tokens or personal information that is not needed to investigate your issue.

3. Why we use the information

We use this information to communicate with you, resolve support requests, investigate reported bugs, follow up on previous issues and protect the community service from abuse. We do not sell ticket information or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Storage and access

Ticket messages and their associated records are kept on our Discord server. In the current setup, ManaFoldBOT does not maintain a separate ticket database or an external archive of ticket contents. Keeping records on Discord still involves processing information through the bot and access by the team handling support.

Ticket visibility depends on Discord channel permissions. Team members with the relevant support or administrative permissions can access the records. We do not publish private ticket contents as part of the support service. Discord hosts and processes information on its infrastructure under its own Privacy Policy.

Support emails are handled in our support mailbox using our email provider, Google. Its handling of information is described in Google's Privacy Policy. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where you specifically ask us to share it.

5. How long information is kept

There is currently no fixed retention period or automatic expiration for ticket records on our Discord server. Records may remain after a ticket is closed or an issue is resolved, so that we can follow up on support requests and investigate recurring problems.

A lack of an automatic expiration does not remove your deletion rights. We delete information under our control when it is no longer needed for the purposes described here, when required by applicable law or Discord's requirements, or following an applicable deletion request. We may retain information that the law requires us to keep.

Related email correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and follow-up. Discord and Google have their own retention practices for data they control, including their infrastructure records and backups.

6. Access, correction and deletion requests

Email manafoldstudio@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information handled by our team. Include your Discord user ID and enough information to identify the relevant ticket, if available. We may ask for a proportionate confirmation that the request concerns your account; never send your Discord password or an authentication token.

We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of your information, or complain to a data-protection authority. Deleting records that we control does not necessarily remove copies independently controlled by Discord or another recipient.

7. Document hosting

When these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or contact forms. See GitHub Pages data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the relevant services or data practices change and revise the date shown above. Material changes will be communicated through our Discord server. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to manafoldstudio@gmail.com.
